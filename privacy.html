Matchy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Matchy ("we", "us") is a mobile app in which people swipe through and chat with AI-generated companion profiles. This policy explains what data the app and its social media channels handle.

Data the app stores

Your swipes, matches and chat messages are stored only on your device. We do not operate user accounts and do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location or photos from you.

AI companions

All profiles in Matchy are fictional and AI-generated. Messages you send may be processed by an AI model to generate replies. We do not sell or share your messages.

Social media

Our TikTok and Instagram channels publish AI-generated "Guess the match" videos. When you interact with those channels, the respective platform's privacy policy applies. Our developer integrations with TikTok and Meta are used only to publish content to our own accounts; they do not access other users' data.

Analytics and third parties

The app does not include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics.

Children

Matchy is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact
